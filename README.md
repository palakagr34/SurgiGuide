SurgiGuide 
SurgiGuide is a mobile application designed to help patients navigate the often overwhelming process of undergoing surgery. 
It provides easy-to-understand resources, planning tools, and an AI-powered chatbot to support patients before and after their surgical procedures. 

Features
- Procedure Overviews: Clear, accessible explanations of common surgical procedures.
- Pre/Post-Op Task Management: Tools to help patients track appointments, medications, and recovery checklists.
- AI Chatbot: A conversational assistant that offers generalized guidance on symptoms, nutrition, and recovery exercises (with disclaimers encouraging medical consultation).

Limitations 
Due to privacy concerns (e.g., HIPAA compliance), the chatbot does not use real patient data and does not provide personalized medical advice. All information is generalized and educational in nature.
